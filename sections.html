Reference

Sections and blocks

What each section and block is for, every setting it has, and tips for using it. Setting names match the theme editor.

Many sections share these settings, so they're described once here:

  • Color scheme: picks one of the color schemes from Theme settings > Colors.
  • Top padding and Bottom padding (under Padding): space above and below the section, from 0 to 100px in steps of 4px.
  • Columns on desktop and Columns on mobile: how many cards or images sit side by side.

Header

The top of every page: logo, navigation, search, account, country and language selectors and cart. It lives in the header group, with the announcement bar.

Header settings
SettingWhat it does
Logo textText logo in the header. Leave empty to show the store name. Hidden when a logo image is set in Theme settings.
Main menuThe menu for the header navigation and the mobile menu.Default: Main menu
Customer account menuThe links in the account menu that opens from the account icon. The icon shows only when customer accounts are turned on.Default: Customer account main menu
Desktop navigation
Navigation typeDropdown shows level 2 links in a dropdown with level 3 links indented under them. Mega menu shows level 2 links as columns in a full-width panel and supports promo blocks.Default: Dropdown
Open navigation on hoverOpens dropdowns and mega menus when the pointer rests on a menu item. Clicking and the keyboard always work.Default: on
Hover delayHow long the pointer rests before a menu opens, from 0 to 600 ms. Shows when Open navigation on hover is on.Default: 150 ms

Show country selector on desktopAdds a compact country and currency selector next to the account and cart icons from 1280px wide.Default: off
Show language selector on desktopAdds a compact language selector next to the account and cart icons from 1280px wide.Default: off

Selectors show only when the store has more than one country or language. The mobile menu always includes them.

A scrolling bar above the header for short messages, such as your shipping offer. Add up to 6 Announcement blocks. With more than one, a separator sits between the messages. The bar pauses when the pointer is over it or a link in it has keyboard focus, and it stays static for customers who turn on reduced motion.

Before and after image

Two images on top of each other with a divider that visitors drag to compare them, for example a stock desk and the same desk with a full RGB setup. Visitors can drag or tap anywhere on the images with a mouse, finger or pen, or use the arrow keys. On phones, swiping up and down still scrolls the page. Add it from the Banners category.

HeadingHeading above the images.Default: See the upgrade

After imageShown on the right side of the divider, at the same size and ratio.
Image ratioAdapt to before image, 16:9, 4:3 or Square. Both images are cropped to this ratio and never stretched.Default: 16:9
Slider

After labelLabel in the top right corner.Default: After
Show hint animationMoves the slider a little once when the section comes into view. Never plays for visitors who prefer reduced motion, or after a visitor moves the slider.Default: On
Button
Button labelOptional button under the images. Leave empty to hide the button.
Button linkPage the button links to. The button only shows when it has a label and a link.
Color schemeColors of the section.

Tips: use two photos taken from the same spot so the comparison lines up. Add alt text to each image in Content > Files. Without alt text, the labels are used. The section only shows in your store when both images are selected. The section also has Top padding and Bottom padding.

  • App blocks: click Add block and pick a block from an installed app, such as a reviews widget.

Cart drawer and cart page

Choose between them in Theme settings > Cart > Cart type. Both show line items with options, subscription details, unit prices and discounts, the subtotal, the estimated total, a tax note and the cart note when it's on. Customers enter discount codes at checkout.

Cart drawer

A side panel that opens after a product is added, from the product page, featured product and quick add on product cards. Customers can change quantities and remove items without leaving the page. Its colors come from Drawer color scheme in Theme settings > Cart. The drawer has no section settings.

404 page

The Not found section shows when a page doesn't exist: a heading, a short message, a search form and a Continue shopping button. It has no settings. You can add other sections to the 404 template, such as a product carousel.

Gift card

The page customers see when they open a gift card they received. It shows your logo or store name, the gift card balance and expiry date, the code with a copy button, a QR code, an Add to Apple Wallet button, a print button and a link to your store. It has no settings and uses your color schemes and logo from Theme settings.

Password page

The page visitors see while your store is password protected. It shows your logo or store name, the message you set in Online Store > Preferences, a password form, an email signup form and the Powered by Shopify link. The Password section has no settings.
