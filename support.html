Help

Support

Need help with Glowcore? Check the FAQ first. If your question isn't answered there, contact us and we'll help.

Contact us

Email us at denkothemes@gmail.com.

What to include

Include these details so we can help you on the first reply:

  • Your name and the email address we should reply to.
  • A subject that sums up the problem in a few words, for example "Mega menu not showing on desktop".
  • Your store URL, for example storename.myshopify.com. If the theme isn't published, also send a preview link.
  • A description of the problem: what you expected, what happens instead, and the page where it happens.
  • Screenshots or a short screen recording showing the problem.
  • The device and browser you saw it on, for example an iPhone with Safari or a Windows laptop with Chrome.

Never send us your Shopify password. If we need access to your store, we'll send a collaborator request that you can review and approve in your Shopify admin.

Reply time and support hours

  • Reply time

    We reply within 2 business days.

  • Support hours

    Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 18:00 Europe/Sofia time (EET/EEST). No support on Bulgarian public holidays. Messages sent outside these hours are answered on the next business day.
