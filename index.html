Glowcore: a neon theme for gaming and tech stores

Glowcore is a dark Shopify theme with glass panels and pink and violet glow accents. It is built for gaming and tech gear stores with large catalogs, including dropshipping stores that sell many products from many collections.

Get started Contact support

Who Glowcore is for

  • Gaming and tech stores

    Peripherals, PC parts, RGB setups, consoles and accessories. The dark look and glow accents suit products that light up.

  • Large catalogs

    Filters and sorting on collection and search pages, a mega menu with three menu levels, predictive search and pagination help customers find products quickly.

  • Dropshippers

    Quick add on product cards, a cart drawer, trust bar and announcement bar for your real shipping and returns terms, and related and complementary products.

Key features

  • Home page sections: video banner, hero, product carousel, category tiles, trust bar, featured collection, featured product, blog posts, image gallery, testimonials and email signup.
  • Navigation: dropdown or mega menu on desktop with up to three menu levels and optional promo images, and a mobile menu drawer.
  • Product page: 19 reorderable blocks, including variant picker with swatches, purchase options for subscriptions, pickup availability, gift card recipient form, collapsible tabs, popup, inventory status and product rating, plus app blocks.
  • Product media: images, videos, YouTube and Vimeo videos and 3D models, with optional image zoom. The selected variant's media moves to the front.
  • Cart: cart drawer or cart page, cart note, discounts, unit prices, subscription details and accelerated checkout buttons.
  • Filtering and sorting: collection and search pages with a vertical sidebar or horizontal bar on desktop and a filter drawer on mobile, powered by the Search and Discovery app.
  • Selling internationally: country and language selectors in the header, mobile menu and footer.
  • Customer features: the Shopify customer account menu in the header, email signup, and the Follow on Shop button.
  • Product ratings: stars on the product page and product cards from a product reviews app. Glowcore never shows ratings that don't exist.
  • Accessibility: keyboard support, visible focus states, a skip link and support for reduced motion.
