Version 1.0.0 2026

Initial release.

  • Dark Glowcore design with glass panels, pink and violet glow accents and two color schemes.
  • Header with dropdown or mega menu (up to three levels), mega menu promo blocks, predictive search, customer account menu and country and language selectors.
  • Home page sections: video banner, hero, product carousel, category tiles, trust bar, featured collection, featured product, blog posts, image gallery, testimonials and email signup.
  • Product page with 19 blocks and app blocks, media gallery with video and 3D models, swatches, purchase options, pickup availability and gift card recipient form.
  • Cart drawer or cart page with cart note, discounts and accelerated checkout buttons.
  • Collection and search pages with filters, sorting and pagination.
  • Blog, blog post, page, collections list, 404, gift card and password templates.
  • Footer with menus, brand, email signup, social media icons, Follow on Shop, payment icons and policy links.
  • Product ratings from product reviews apps, on the product page and product cards.
  • Apps, Custom Liquid and Custom section sections for apps and advanced customization.
