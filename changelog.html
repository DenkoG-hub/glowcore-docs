Changelog

What's new in each version of Glowcore. To update, see How do I update Glowcore?

Version 1.0.0 2026

Initial release of Glowcore, a dark theme with glass panels and pink and violet glow accents, built for gaming and tech stores with large catalogs.

Layout and navigation

  • Header with logo text or logo image, main menu and customer account menu.
  • Navigation type: Dropdown or Mega menu, with menus up to three levels deep. Option to open navigation on hover, with a hover delay.
  • Mega menu promo blocks with image, heading and link, shown in the mega menu of a chosen menu item.
  • Mobile menu drawer with nested menus and country and language selectors.
  • Predictive search in the header with products, collections, articles and pages.
  • Announcement bar with multiple announcements in a scrolling bar, optional links and an optional truck icon. Pauses on hover and focus.
  • Footer with Menu, Text, Brand, Email signup, Social media icons and Custom Liquid blocks, maximum columns on desktop, social media icons in the bottom row, payment icons, policy links and a Powered by Shopify link.
  • Custom Liquid blocks in the header and footer for app snippets.

Home page sections

  • Video banner: muted looping video with heading, subheading, button, text position, overlay strength and height. Visitors can pause it.
  • Hero: background image with heading, subheading and button.
  • Product carousel: scrolling product row from a collection, with previous and next buttons and an optional "View all" button.
  • Category tiles: collection tiles with optional custom image and custom label.
  • Trust bar: trust items with shipping, secure checkout, returns or support icons.
  • Featured collection: product grid from a collection with an optional "View all" button.
  • Featured product: a single product with the same blocks as the product page.
  • Featured blog: posts from a blog with image, date, author and excerpt options.
  • Image gallery: image grid with captions, links, or a product link that shows the product title and price on hover or focus.
  • Before and after image: two images compared with a draggable divider, with heading, text, labels, starting position, image ratio, an optional button and a one-time hint animation. Works with mouse, touch, pen and keyboard.
  • Testimonials: customer quotes with author name, author detail, author image and an optional rating.
  • Email signup: newsletter form.
  • Contact form, Apps, Custom Liquid and Custom section (with Group and Text blocks) can be added to any page.

Product page

  • Product information section with these blocks: Title, Vendor, Text, Price, Variant picker, Quantity selector, Buy buttons, Purchase options, Pickup availability, Gift card recipient form, Description, Collapsible tab, SKU, Inventory status, Share, Popup, Custom Liquid, Complementary products, Product rating and app blocks.
  • Media gallery with thumbnails, swipe, images, videos, external videos and 3D models (with the view in your space button). Optional image zoom in a lightbox. Desktop media width setting.
  • Variant picker as buttons or a dropdown, with optional swatches from the product admin.
  • Dynamic checkout buttons, subscription options from selling plans, and gift card recipient form on gift card products.
  • Inventory status uses real inventory, with an optional count when low.
  • Product rating shows ratings saved by a product reviews app, with an optional link to reviews.
  • Related products section with recommendations from Shopify's Search and Discovery app.
  • Separate gift card product template.

Collections and search

  • Collection and Search sections with filters from the Search and Discovery app, sorting and pagination.
  • Filter layout on desktop: vertical sidebar or horizontal bar. Filters open in a drawer on mobile.
  • Active filter pills and a clear all option. Settings for visible values per filter, products per page and columns on desktop and mobile.
  • Collection banner with optional collection image and description.
  • Search results include products, articles and pages.
  • Collections list with sort options, image ratio, product count, collection description and an option to hide empty collections.
  • Product cards with glass styling, second image on hover, sale and new badges, color swatches, quick add and optional product rating.

Cart

  • Cart type: Drawer or Page.
  • Cart drawer updates without reloading the page and has its own color scheme setting.
  • Optional cart note.
  • Line item and cart discounts, unit prices, and an estimated total with a tax and shipping note.
  • Dynamic checkout buttons on the cart page.
  • App blocks and Custom Liquid in the cart footer.

Content pages

  • Blog posts: grid or list layout, tag filter, and options for featured image, date, author, excerpt and tags.
  • Blog post: featured image, title, date and author, content, share links, tags, back to blog link, previous and next posts, and comments.
  • Page: content width, heading alignment and an optional glass panel. Supports app blocks and Custom Liquid.
  • 404 page with search and a link back to products.
  • Gift card page with QR code, Apple Wallet link and print styles.
  • Password page with store message and email signup.

Store features

  • Country and language selectors in the header (desktop, optional), the mobile menu and the footer. They show only when the store has more than one country or language.
  • Email signup in the Email signup section, the footer and the password page. Subscribers are added to Customers with email marketing consent.
  • Follow on Shop button in the footer.
  • Customer accounts through Shopify's account menu in the header, with sign in and a customer account menu.
  • Social media links for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Discord and Twitch.

Accessibility and performance

  • Skip to content link, visible focus styles and keyboard-accessible menus, drawers and dialogs.
  • Animations and scrolling content respect the reduced motion setting.
  • Built with native browser features such as details and dialog, with small deferred scripts and no external libraries.
  • Responsive images with lazy loading below the fold.
  • Social sharing and search engine meta tags.

Customization

  • Color schemes with background, text, secondary text, icons, primary and secondary buttons, primary button glow, accent, accent background and borders. Two schemes included. Most sections have a color scheme setting.
  • Typography: font for headings and body text from the Shopify font library.
  • Layout: page width, page margin and input corner radius.
  • Logo and favicon: logo image, logo width and favicon image. Without a logo image, the header shows an animated text logo.
  • Product cards: show product rating.
  • Cart: cart type, cart note and drawer color scheme.
  • Social media: links to social media accounts.
  • Top and bottom padding settings on most sections.
