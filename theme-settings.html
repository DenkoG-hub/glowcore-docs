Theme settings

Theme settings apply to the whole store. In the theme editor, click the gear icon to open them.

Setting groups

  1. Logo and favicon
  2. Typography
  3. Layout
  4. Colors
  5. Product cards
  6. Cart
  7. Social media

Logo and favicon

Logo and favicon settings
SettingWhat it does
Logo imageReplaces the animated text logo in the header. Leave empty to keep the text logo.
Logo widthWidth of the logo image in the header, from 50 to 300px.Default: 140px
Favicon imageThe icon in the browser tab and bookmarks. Use a square image, 32 x 32px recommended.

The footer's Brand block uses this logo too, unless you pick a different image in that block.

Typography

Typography settings, under the Fonts heading
SettingWhat it does
Heading and body textThe font for all headings and body text. Pick any font from the Shopify font library. Bold and italic styles load automatically.Default: Work Sans

Layout

Layout settings
SettingWhat it does
Page widthMaximum width of page content. Narrow or Wide.Default: Narrow
Page marginMinimum space between the content and the edges of the screen, from 10 to 100px.Default: 20px
Input corner radiusCorner rounding of form fields, such as the search field, email signup and quantity inputs, from 0 to 10px.Default: 4px

Colors

Colors are organized in color schemes. Edit a scheme here, then choose it in any section's Color scheme setting. Glowcore comes with Scheme 1 (near-black, pink buttons) and Scheme 2 (dark gray, violet buttons). You can add more schemes.

Colors in each color scheme
SettingWhat it does
BackgroundBackground color of sections that use the scheme.
Background gradientOptional second background color. Glowcore 1.0.0 doesn't use it yet, so you can leave it empty.
TextHeadings and body text.
Secondary textLess prominent text, such as subheadings, dates, vendor names, product counts and helper text.
IconsIcons in the footer and in the filters.
Primary button backgroundFill of primary buttons, such as Add to cart, Quick add and Shop now.
Primary button textText on primary buttons.
Primary button borderBorder of primary buttons.
Primary button glowThe soft neon glow around primary buttons and other glowing elements. Kept separate from the button background so the button text stays readable.
Secondary button backgroundFill of secondary buttons.
Secondary button textText on secondary buttons.
Secondary button borderBorder of secondary buttons.
AccentLinks, highlights and active states.
Accent backgroundSolid accent fill behind small labels, such as the New badge on product cards and the count badges in filters. Its text uses the Primary button text color.
BordersBorders and dividers of cards, panels and form fields.

The Sale badge on product cards uses the primary button color. Keep the Primary button text color readable on both the Primary button background and the Accent background.

Product cards

Product card settings
SettingWhat it does
Show product ratingShows stars from the reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count product metafields, usually filled in by a product reviews app. Products without a rating show no stars.Default: off

Product cards appear in collections, search results, product carousels, featured collections and related products. A card shows a Sale badge when the product has a compare-at price higher than its price, or a New badge when the product was created in the last 30 days.

Cart

Cart settings
SettingWhat it does
Cart typeDrawer opens a side panel after a product is added. Page goes to the cart page.Default: Drawer
Show cart noteAdds a note field to the cart drawer and cart page, so customers can leave instructions with their order.Default: on
Drawer color schemeThe color scheme of the cart drawer.Default: Scheme 1

Social media

Under Social media accounts, paste the full URL of each profile, for example the address of your Instagram page. The footer's Social media icons block shows an icon only for the links you fill in.

Social media settings
SettingWhat it does
FacebookLink to your Facebook page.
InstagramLink to your Instagram profile.
TikTokLink to your TikTok profile.
XLink to your X profile.
YouTubeLink to your YouTube channel.
DiscordInvite link to your Discord server.
TwitchLink to your Twitch channel.
