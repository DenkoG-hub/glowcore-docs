Install from the Theme Store

  1. Open the Glowcore listing in the Shopify Theme Store while you're logged in to your store.
  2. Click Try theme to add Glowcore to your theme library for free, or buy it to use it on your live store.
  3. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes. Glowcore appears in the theme library and isn't published yet, so your customers don't see it.

Installing a theme never changes your products, collections, orders or customers. Only the look of your online store changes, and only after you publish.

Preview the theme

In Online Store > Themes, find Glowcore in the theme library, click the … (actions) button and choose Preview. The preview shows Glowcore with your own products and content. You can share the preview link with others before you publish.

Customize in the theme editor

Click Customize next to Glowcore to open the theme editor.

  • Page selector: use the menu at the top of the editor to switch between the home page, product pages, collection pages, the cart and other templates.
  • Sections and blocks: the sidebar lists the header, the page's sections and the footer. Click a section to edit its settings, drag sections and blocks to reorder them, and use Add section or Add block to add more. See Sections and blocks.
  • Theme settings: click the gear icon to change the logo, fonts, layout, colors, product cards, cart and social media links. See Theme settings.
  • Save: click Save to keep your changes. Saving an unpublished theme doesn't change your live store.

The Glowcore home page starts with these sections: Video banner, Hero, Product carousel, Trust bar, Category tiles, Featured collection, Blog posts, a second Product carousel and Image gallery. Pick a collection, blog and images for each one, or remove the sections you don't need.

Publish the theme

  1. Go to Online Store > Themes.
  2. Find Glowcore in the theme library, click the … (actions) button and choose Publish.
  3. Confirm. Glowcore becomes your live theme, and your previous theme stays in the theme library so you can switch back.

Set up menus

Menus are managed in your Shopify admin under Content > Menus. Glowcore uses three menus:

Menus used by Glowcore
MenuWhere it shows
Main menu (main-menu)The header navigation on desktop and the mobile menu drawer. Selected by default in the header's Main menu setting.
Footer menu (footer)The footer's Menu blocks. Selected by default in each new Menu block.
Customer account menu (customer-account-main-menu)The links inside the account menu that opens from the account icon in the header. Selected by default in the header's Customer account menu setting.

Dropdown menus with three levels

To create a dropdown, drag a menu item onto another item in Content > Menus, so it's nested under it. Glowcore shows up to three levels:

  • Level 1: the items in the header bar.
  • Level 2: the links in the dropdown, or the column headings in the mega menu.
  • Level 3: the links listed under each level 2 link.

For large catalogs, nest collections by type, for example PC parts > Graphics cards or Peripherals > Keyboards. See How do I set up the mega menu?

Choose the header menus

  1. In the theme editor, click Header.
  2. Under Main menu, pick the menu for the header navigation.
  3. Under Customer account menu, pick the menu for the account menu.
  4. Under Desktop navigation, choose a Navigation type: Dropdown or Mega menu.

The desktop navigation shows from 1280px wide. On smaller screens, the menu opens from the menu button as a drawer.

Logo and favicon

In the theme editor, open Theme settings > Logo and favicon.

  • Logo image: replaces the animated text logo in the header. Leave it empty to keep the text logo.
  • Logo width: sets the width of the logo image (50 to 300px).
  • Favicon image: the small icon in the browser tab. Use a square image, 32 x 32px recommended.

Without a logo image, the header shows the text from the header's Logo text setting, or your store name when that setting is empty.

Color schemes

Glowcore comes with two color schemes. Edit them in Theme settings > Colors, then pick a scheme in a section's Color scheme setting.

  • Scheme 1: near-black background, pink primary buttons and a violet accent.
  • Scheme 2: dark gray background, violet primary buttons and a pink accent.

Each scheme has its own background, text, button, accent and border colors. See Colors for what each color does.

Keep text and buttons readable when you change colors. Text should have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background.

Free shipping text in the announcement bar and trust bar

The announcement bar and the trust bar come with free shipping text as an example. Glowcore doesn't read your shipping rates, so change the text to match your real rates in Settings > Shipping and delivery.

  • Announcement bar: in the theme editor, open the header group, click Announcement bar and then the Announcement block. Change Text (the default is "Free shipping on orders over $50") and add a Link if you want. Turn Show truck icon off if your message isn't about shipping.
  • Trust bar: click Trust bar on the home page, then each Trust item block. Change the Icon, Heading (default "Free shipping") and Subheading (default "On all orders").

Only promise what your store offers. If you don't offer free shipping, replace the text with a true statement, like your delivery times or returns window.

Policies

Add your refund, privacy, terms of service, shipping and contact policies in Settings > Policies. The footer links to every policy you add when Show policy links is on in the footer section.

Markets and languages for the country and language selectors

Glowcore shows a country selector and a language selector only when there's something to choose:

  • Country selector: add more than one country or region in Settings > Markets. The selector lists each country with its currency.
  • Language selector: add and publish more than one language in Settings > Languages.

Where the selectors show:

  • Footer: controlled by Show country selector and Show language selector in the footer section (on by default).
  • Header on desktop: controlled by Show country selector on desktop and Show language selector on desktop in the header section (off by default).
  • Mobile menu: always included when the store has more than one country or language.

Email signup and customer accounts

Email signup

Glowcore has two email signup forms: the Email signup section, and the Email signup block in the footer. When a customer subscribes, Shopify adds them to Customers with the newsletter tag and email marketing consent. You can then email them with Shopify Email or another email marketing app.

Customer accounts

The account icon in the header shows when customer accounts are turned on in Settings > Customer accounts. Customers use it to sign in and to open the account menu. The links in that menu come from the Customer account menu setting in the header. When customer accounts are off, the account icon is hidden.

Follow on Shop

The footer can show a Follow on Shop button so customers can follow your store in the Shop app. It needs:

  • Shop Pay turned on in Settings > Payments.
  • The Shop channel installed on your store.
  • Show Follow on Shop button turned on in the footer section (on by default).

When these aren't met, Shopify doesn't output the button and the footer shows nothing in its place.
