How do I show color swatches?

Swatches in the variant picker come from Shopify's color swatches. Connect your Color option to the Color category metafield:

  1. Open the product in your Shopify admin and choose a Category that has a Color attribute, for example a product category for keyboards or headsets.
  2. Under Variants, edit the Color option and connect it to the Color metafield.
  3. Pick the color entries for each value. You can set a color or an image for each entry in Content > Metaobjects.
  4. In the theme editor, make sure Show swatches is on in the product page's Variant picker block.

Options that aren't connected to a metafield show as plain buttons or a dropdown, depending on the block's Style. Product cards show small thumbnails of up to five variant images instead of swatches.

How do I set up filters?

Filters on collection and search pages are managed in Shopify's free Search and Discovery app:

  1. Install the Search and Discovery app.
  2. Open the app, go to Filters and add the filters you want, such as availability, price, product type, vendor, color or size.
  3. In the theme editor, make sure Show filters is on in the Collection and Search sections, and choose a Filter layout on desktop.

Filters based on the Color metafield show as color swatches.

How do I show product ratings?

Glowcore shows stars only when a product has a real rating. Ratings must come from a product reviews app that writes Shopify's standard product metafields reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count. Check your reviews app's documentation to see if it does.

  1. Install a product reviews app and turn on its option to sync ratings to Shopify metafields, if it has one.
  2. On the product page, add the Product rating block to Product information.
  3. To show stars on product cards, turn on Show product rating in Theme settings > Product cards.
  4. Optional: turn on Link to reviews in the Product rating block. It links to the element with the ID reviews, for example your reviews app's block.

Products without a rating show no stars. Glowcore never shows placeholder or sample ratings.

How do I show a different image for each variant?
  1. Open the product in your Shopify admin and upload all the images to the product's Media.
  2. Under Variants, click a variant, then click its image placeholder and choose the matching image.
  3. Repeat for each variant and save.

When a customer picks a variant on the product page, its image moves to the front of the gallery. Product cards always show the product's first image, and cards for products with several variants also show small thumbnails of up to five variant images.

How do I set up the mega menu?
  1. In Content > Menus, open your main menu and nest items to build up to three levels. Level 2 items become the columns, level 3 items are listed under them.
  2. In the theme editor, click Header and set Navigation type to Mega menu.
  3. Optional: add a Mega menu promo block. In Menu item, type the exact name of the top-level menu item, for example Peripherals, then add an image, heading and link.

The mega menu shows on screens 1280px wide and up. On smaller screens, the same menu opens in the mobile menu drawer as expandable lists.

Why don't the country or language selectors show?

Each selector only shows when there's more than one option to choose:

  • Country selector: needs more than one country or region in Settings > Markets.
  • Language selector: needs more than one published language in Settings > Languages.

Then check the settings: Show country selector and Show language selector in the footer section, and Show country selector on desktop and Show language selector on desktop in the header section (off by default). The mobile menu always includes them.

Why doesn't the Follow on Shop button show?

The button needs all of these:

  • Shop Pay turned on in Settings > Payments.
  • The Shop channel installed on your store.
  • Show Follow on Shop button turned on in the footer section.

Shopify decides when to output the button. When it doesn't, the footer leaves no empty space.

Should I use the cart drawer or the cart page?

Choose in Theme settings > Cart > Cart type.

  • Drawer (default): customers stay on the page after adding a product and can keep shopping. Good for large catalogs where customers add several products.
  • Page: customers go to the cart page after adding a product. Good if you want every customer to review the cart before checkout.

Both show the same information, and the cart page is always available at /cart.

How do I update Glowcore?

When a new version is available, Shopify shows an update notice for Glowcore in Online Store > Themes.

  1. Click the update notice and review what's new in the changelog.
  2. Add the update. Shopify adds the new version to your theme library as an unpublished copy, with the settings and content you set in the theme editor.
  3. Preview the updated copy, check your pages, then publish it.

Changes you made to the theme code aren't carried over to the update. Keep a note of them, or a duplicate of your theme, before you update.

How do I translate the theme?

Glowcore's storefront text is in English.

  • To change the wording in English: go to Online Store > Themes, click the … (actions) button next to Glowcore and choose Edit default theme content. Search for the text, for example "Add to cart", and change it.
  • To sell in other languages: add and publish the languages in Settings > Languages, then translate the theme text and your content with a translation app, such as Shopify's Translate and Adapt app.

Where do customers enter discount codes?

At checkout. The cart doesn't have a discount code field. Discounts that are already applied, such as automatic discounts or codes from a discount link, show in the cart drawer and on the cart page.

Can I edit the theme code?

Yes, but custom code changes aren't covered by support. Before you edit code, duplicate your theme in Online Store > Themes so you can go back. For custom work, we recommend hiring a Shopify Partner. Try the Custom Liquid section or block first, since it doesn't change the theme files.
